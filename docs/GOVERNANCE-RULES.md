# Governance rule base (Saudi Arabia)

Sources behind the agenda item review (`src/lib/item-review.ts`) and the standard item wordings (`src/lib/item-templates.ts`).
Article numbers were checked against the official English translations; the Arabic text is binding.

- CL: Companies Law, Royal Decree M/132 (1/12/1443H), https://misa.gov.sa/app/uploads/2025/07/Companies-Law.pdf
- CGR: CMA Corporate Governance Regulations (amended 2023), https://cma.org.sa
- IR: CMA Implementing Regulation of the Companies Law for Listed JSCs (consolidated 2026), https://cma.gov.sa/en/RulesRegulations/Regulations/Documents/Implementing_Regulation_of_the_Companies_Law_for_Listed_Joint_Stock_Companies_en.pdf
- OSCOs: CMA Rules on the Offer of Securities and Continuing Obligations (2024)

## Conflicts and related parties
- CL 27: no direct/indirect interest in company contracts, and no competing business, without general assembly authorization.
- CL 71: immediate disclosure to the board, recorded in minutes; no vote at board or assembly; assembly informed with a special auditor's report.
- CL 96(2): each interested contract is a separate assembly item.
- CL 72: no loans or loan guarantees to board members (contract void).
- CGR 42: conflicted member excluded from deliberation and vote. CGR 44: competing business, assessed annually.
- IR 64 (listed): assembly may delegate authorization to the board for one year when a deal is < 1% of revenue and < SAR 10m, ordinary course, no preferential terms.
- OSCOs 80: related-party deal >= 1% of revenue disclosed immediately.

## General assembly matters
- Extraordinary (CL 85): bylaws, capital increase/decrease (CL 127/134), dissolution/continuation, buy-back (CL 114, IR 17), mergers (CL 225).
- Ordinary (CL 87): board election/removal, auditor appointment and fees, board report and financial statements, dividends (CL 125), board remuneration (CL 76), sale of > 50% of assets (CL 75), removal of absent members (CL 70).
- Interim dividends: CL 22; listed: articles + annual assembly authorization + liquidity (IR 47).
- Annual ordinary assembly within 6 months of year end (CL 88), 21 days' notice (CL 91).
- Losses of half the capital: announce within 60 days, extraordinary assembly within 180 days (CL 132).

## Board meetings
- At least 4 a year (CL 80/1); listed: at least one per quarter (CGR 30).
- Notice 5 days with agenda and papers (CGR 30). Quorum: half the members (CL 80/2); listed: at least 3 present.
- Majority of those present; chair's casting vote (CL 80/3). Proxy only to another member, one each (CL 81).
- Circular resolutions for urgent matters, recorded at the next meeting (CL 82).
- Minutes signed by chair, members present and secretary, kept in a register (CL 83); retained 10 years (CGR 93).
- Secretary appointed and removed by the board (CL 78/4; CGR 35); qualifications (CGR 36, guiding).
- Vacancy: temporary appointment, notified within 15 days, presented to the next ordinary assembly (CL 69).

## Committees (listed)
- 3-5 members, CMA notified within 5 days (CGR 47); board chair not on audit committee nor chairing statutory committees (CGR 48).
- Audit committee: no executive members, a finance/accounting specialist, at least 4 meetings a year; reviews statements before the board and recommends the auditor (CGR 51-54).
- Auditor term at most 7 consecutive years (IR 3).

## Reporting deadlines (listed)
- Interim statements within 30 days; annual within 3 months and 21 days before the assembly (OSCOs 81).
- Board report contents: CGR 87.
